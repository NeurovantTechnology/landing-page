What we’re asking

Can a digital tool help without becoming another thing to manage?

We’re looking at the space between attention, movement, and the routines people already have. The question comes before the feature list.

Right now, we’re talking through problems and sketching rough prototypes. There’s nothing to download, and we don’t have a launch date to share.

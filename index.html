Current experiment / Neurofit

Can a tool make room for one more good habit?

We're starting with a practical question: can digital support for attention and movement fit around a person's day instead of taking it over?

The problem

Screen-heavy days make it easy to lose track of attention and movement.

We keep coming back to the gap between knowing a routine would help and finding a way to make room for it.

What we're learning

We're talking through the problem, sketching rough prototypes, and deciding whether there is a small, useful tool here. Neurofit is an early exploration: there is nothing to download and no finished product yet.

What we believe

Technology should
fit into your day.

Neurovant is two founders exploring small, practical ways technology might support attention, movement, and everyday routines.

We care less about adding more to a tool and more about whether it earns a place in a real routine. If it creates another demand, it needs a good reason to be there.

How we work

Three checks before
we keep going.

This is the order we try to follow when an idea starts to feel tempting.

  1. 01

    Ask first.

    Start with the problem a person actually has, not the feature we want to build.

    Then ask what matters
  2. 02

    Earn its place.

    A product should make one part of a day easier. Otherwise, it's just more noise.

    Then keep it small
  3. 03

    People decide.

    The person using the tool should understand what it does and choose how to use it.

    Then let it go
